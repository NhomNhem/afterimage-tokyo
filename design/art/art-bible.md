# Glass Refrain Art Bible (M0/Sprint 2 Baseline)

## 1. Visual Identity Foundation

### One-line Visual Rule
Melancholic, rain-soaked duel framing where readable combat truth is always clearer than spectacle.

### Supporting Principles
- Readability first for enemy intent, player action beats, and lock-on framing.
- Restrained contrast and effects; avoid clutter that hides combat phases.
- Emotional tone through environment, lighting, and pacing rather than heavy VFX spam.

## 2. World and Mood Direction

- Mood: Sad, mysterious, restrained, elegant.
- Primary setting: Tokyo street prototype duel space under wet/cool conditions.
- Memory distortion is represented with subtle shifts, not hard visual overload.

## 3. Character and Enemy Readability

- Player silhouette remains clean during LightAttack / Dodge / Parry.
- Enemy telegraph must be visually distinct in Telegraph/Commit/Active/Recovery.
- Counter and reveal placeholders should communicate state change without occluding intent.

## 4. Camera and Composition Rules

- Keep both player and locked enemy readable in frame during core duel beats.
- Favor stable framing through defensive windows.
- Camera is presentation/framing only and must not imply gameplay authority.

## 5. Color and Lighting Guidelines

- Palette: cool rain-neutral base with selective accent colors for intent and state transitions.
- Telegraph/counter/reveal accents must be separable at a glance.
- Avoid saturated full-screen effects during core combat exchanges.

## 6. VFX Principles (M0 Placeholder)

- VFX role: confirm and clarify, never decide gameplay.
- Reveal VFX: short, restrained, low-obstruction.
- Parry/counter feedback: brief and directional; no persistent full-screen overlays.

## 7. UI and Debug Visual Language

- Debug Overlay remains read-only and legible.
- Debug text/layout should prioritize quick scan over visual polish.
- Overlay content must remain camera-independent truth display.

## 8. Animation Readability Baseline

- Placeholder clips are acceptable if phase readability is preserved.
- Combat phase transitions must be distinguishable even without final polish.
- No animation-only ownership of combat outcomes.

## 9. Production Boundaries for M0/S2

- Non-goals in this phase:
  - No cinematic camera system
  - No boss presentation pass
  - No full HUD style pass
  - No broad lore/environment expansion
- This art bible is a sprint-ready baseline and should be refined after S2-4/S2-5 verification.
